Smash Burger

Back to Home

Description

The ultimate smash burger with crispy edges, melted American cheese, and a toasted bun.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef (80/20)
  • American cheese
  • Burger buns
  • Salt and pepper
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Form the ground beef into loose balls.
  2. Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Place the beef balls in the skillet and smash them flat with a spatula.
  4. Season with salt and pepper, then flip after 2 minutes.
  5. Add a slice of cheese, cover until melted, and serve on a butter-toasted bun.
