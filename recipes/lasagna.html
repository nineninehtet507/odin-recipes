Classic Lasagna

Back to Home

Description

This is a classic Italian lasagna recipe. It has beautiful layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • Pasta sheets
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a pan and add tomato sauce.
  3. Layer the pasta, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  4. Bake for 45 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
