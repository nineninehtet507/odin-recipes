Homemade Pizza

Description

A simple and delicious homemade pepperoni pizza with a crispy crust and perfectly melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni slices

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 475 degrees.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a baking sheet.
  3. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Add mozzarella cheese and top with pepperoni.
  5. Bake for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden.
